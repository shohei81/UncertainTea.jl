Design Notes

UncertainTea keeps a set of design and reference documents alongside the code. They track the architecture and design rationale in depth and are current with the implementation. The most user-facing material has been adapted into Getting Started and the Inference Overview; the design documents below remain the authoritative deep-dives, and live under docs/ in the repository:

Architecture and DSL

  • architecture.md — the recommended architecture and layering strategy.
  • dsl.md — the minimal DSL proposal, close to official Gen syntax.
  • constraint-driven-conditioning.md — how observations vs. latents are classified from the conditioning signature.

Batched and device execution

  • batched-inference.md — the phased design for batched evaluators and GPU-oriented execution.
  • device-backend.md — device-resident batched logjoint via KernelAbstractions.
  • vector-backend-lowering.md — bringing vector-valued latent families into the device subset.
  • device-vector-latents.md — device-resident vector latents.

Samplers

  • gpu-native-nuts.md — design constraints for iterative, GPU-oriented NUTS.
  • persistent-nuts.md — the persistent per-chain tree kernel (true one-launch GPU-native NUTS).
  • chees-hmc.md — ChEES-HMC, the cross-chain trajectory-length adaptive sampler.
  • mh-within-gibbs.md — Metropolis-within-Gibbs for discrete structure.
  • discrete-enumeration.md — discrete enumeration / marginalization.
  • noncentered-reparam.md — automatic non-centered reparameterization with address preservation.

Benchmarks and research

  • benchmarks.md — cross-PPL correctness + performance results (NumPyro/Stan) and methodology; harness in bench/crossppl/.
  • research.md — ecosystem research behind a GPU-native Julia PPL.
